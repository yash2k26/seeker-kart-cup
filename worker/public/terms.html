Online rooms and paid entry

Online rooms let up to six players race each other. A host may set an entry fee (in SKR or SOL) for a room. Entry fees are paid to our treasury wallet and held until the room's races end. The prize pool is the sum of entries. A platform fee of 10% of the pool is taken at settlement, except in rooms of two (duels) and rooms of six (full grids), where no fee is taken; 20% of any fee is paid to the room's host as a hosting reward. The remaining pool is paid, automatically and on-chain, to the top finishers by the published split (two players: winner takes all; three or four: 65% / 35%; five or six: 60% / 25% / 15%). The pool, the fee and each place's prize are shown in the lobby before you pay. Results are decided by our servers from the finish times each phone reports and validates against the track's limits; a disconnected or timed-out racer is placed last. Entries are refunded in full if you leave before the first race starts or if the room closes before racing begins; after the first race has started there are no refunds. Payouts are sent from our treasury wallet, normally within a minute of the last race, and the transaction signature is shown to every player.

Eligibility. Paid rooms are only for players aged 18 or over (or the age of majority where they live) in places where paid competitions of skill are lawful. Paid rooms are not offered in India, Belgium, Japan, South Korea, the US states of Arkansas, Connecticut, Delaware, Louisiana and South Dakota, or any sanctioned jurisdiction, and we may refuse or refund an entry from anywhere we believe paid play is unlawful. Paid rooms contain no item boxes and no computer-controlled racers: the outcome is decided by driving. You are responsible for any tax on prizes you receive.
