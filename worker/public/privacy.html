Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026.

Kartel ("the game") is an unofficial parody kart racer for Android and the Solana dApp Store. This policy explains what the game collects and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Wallet public key — only if you choose "Save to leaderboard" and sign in with your Solana wallet (Mobile Wallet Adapter). We never see your private key.
  • Race results — lap times, finishing position, the track, and an anonymous device identifier generated by the app so guest races can be saved before sign-in.
  • Seeker verification — whether the wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token (read from the public Solana ledger) and your public .skr name, used to show a badge and a readable name on the leaderboard.
  • On-chain receipts — transaction signatures you sign (personal-best memos, paint-job purchases) so the game can verify them.
  • Usage events and crash reports — race started/finished, wallet connected, and the last error message, to keep the game working. No precise location, no contacts, no advertising identifiers.

Who processes it

Cloudflare (Workers and D1, our backend), a public Solana RPC provider, and the public Solana blockchain for anything you sign. We do not sell data.

Online rooms

Online races run through a Cloudflare Durable Object (a small server dedicated to your room) that relays each player's kart position to the others about fifteen times a second and decides the finishing order. To do that it processes your device's IP address, your wallet address (or an anonymous device id in free rooms), your chosen character and kart, and the race times your phone reports. Cloudflare also tells us the country (and, in the US, the state) a connection comes from; we use that only to refuse paid entry where it is unlawful, and we do not store it. Room records (code, entries, prizes and payout signatures) are kept in our database so payouts can be audited and refunds made. The game has no chat, no free-text input and no user-generated content: room codes are generated by the game and player names are your wallet's short address or public .skr name.

Payments and prizes

Purchases, entry fees and prizes are public Solana transactions. We record their signatures against your wallet address, and the prices we quote are computed from a public price feed (CoinGecko) that we call from our servers, never from your phone.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Settings → Delete my data removes your wallet, results, unlocks and events from our servers immediately. On-chain transactions cannot be deleted by anyone. You can also email REPLACE@example.com.

Children

The Solana dApp Store requires users to be 18 or older; the game is not directed at children.
