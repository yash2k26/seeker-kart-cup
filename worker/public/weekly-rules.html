Kartel Weekly — Official Rules

Last updated 7 October 2026.

No purchase necessary. Free to enter. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.

How it works

Each week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the following Monday 00:00 UTC and features one circuit, shown in the app. To enter, sign in with your wallet and drive the circuit's Kartel Weekly time trial in the app. The time trial is the same for everyone: you are alone on the track, there are no items, no turbo pads and no oil, and every kart has the same performance (nothing bought or earned in the game changes it). You may drive it as often as you like; your single fastest valid lap of the week counts.

Winners

Laps are timed and checked by our servers from the drive your phone records; a run the servers do not accept does not count. The fastest valid lap wins; if two laps are equal, the one set first ranks higher. The three highest-ranked eligible entrants each win the fixed prize shown in the app for their place before the week begins (1st, 2nd and 3rd: 50%, 30% and 20% of the week's prize). Prizes are paid only if at least three eligible entrants drove the time trial that week. The value of SKR in other currencies changes and is not guaranteed.

Eligibility

You must be 18 or older (19 in Alabama and Nebraska, 21 in Mississippi) or the age of majority where you live, and your entry must be made from a wallet holding a Seeker Genesis Token that our servers verified. One prize per Seeker Genesis Token per week; additional wallets or devices are not eligible. Not open to entries made from India, or from Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria or the Crimea, Donetsk or Luhansk regions, or where prohibited by law. Every lap still appears on the board; an ineligible entrant's prize place passes to the next eligible one.

Payment

Prizes are sent in SKR to the winning wallet within 72 hours after the week closes, after the top laps have been reviewed. Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prizes; winners in the United States may be asked for a W-9 before payment when their prizes in a year reach the reporting threshold.

Fair play

Modified apps, automation, multiple accounts or any attempt to submit a time not driven by you lead to disqualification and loss of any prize. The Sponsor may review, remove or disqualify any run, and may cancel or change a week in the event of fraud, a technical failure or anything else that affects its fairness.

Privacy

The board shows your .skr name or a shortened wallet address and your lap. We store the run, its country (to apply the eligibility rules) and your wallet address as described in the privacy notice. The list of winners is shown in the app and is available on request.

Treasure chests

Treasure chests are earned by racing and are never sold. What is inside is for the game only, cannot be transferred and has no cash value. Chests are not part of Kartel Weekly's prizes except as shown in the app.
